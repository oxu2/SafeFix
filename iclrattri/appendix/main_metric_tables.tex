\section{Main-Result Metric Tables}
\label{app:main-metric-tables}

This section reports the original task metrics corresponding to the RER tables in the main text. The metrics are test accuracy for ImageNet10 and CelebA, COCO keypoint AP for pose estimation, and test mAP50--95 for KITTI object detection. All entries report the mean and standard deviation ($\pm$) over seeds 0, 42, 1234, 3407, and 2024. We performed paired t-tests to evaluate statistical significance, confirming that the improvements of \model over the respective best baselines are statistically significant ($p < 0.05$) across the reported selection ratios.
